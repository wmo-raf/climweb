# Publishing your accessibility statement

An accessibility statement tells visitors how accessible your site is, what doesn't work yet, and how to report a problem or ask for information in another format. Many countries require one for public sector websites.

## Add it to your site

1. In the admin, add a new page under your home page using the **Flex Page** type, titled "Accessibility".
2. Paste the template below into it and replace everything in [square brackets].
3. Use your **Feedback** page (or an email address) as the way to report problems.
4. Under **Settings → Navigation settings → Footer menu**, add a link to the new page so it appears on every page.
5. Review the statement at least once a year, and after each ClimWeb upgrade.

Base the "how accessible" and "what doesn't work yet" sections on the [conformance checklist](conformance.md), plus anything specific to your content: videos without captions, scanned PDFs, and images without descriptions.

## Template

> ### Accessibility statement for [site name]
>
> [Organisation name] is committed to making [site address] accessible to as many people as possible, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, magnification or speech recognition.
>
> #### How accessible this website is
>
> This website aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. You should be able to:
>
> - navigate the whole site with a keyboard
> - use the site with a screen reader
> - zoom in up to 400% without text spilling off the screen
> - change the language of the site
> - read all text at a sufficient contrast
>
> #### What isn't fully accessible yet
>
> We know some parts of this website aren't fully accessible:
>
> - [From the conformance checklist, e.g.: Filters on listing pages reload the page as soon as you change them.]
> - [From the conformance checklist, e.g.: Some search box borders have low contrast.]
> - [Your content, e.g.: Some older bulletins are only available as scanned PDFs.]
> - [Your content, e.g.: Some videos don't have captions yet.]
> - Some features come from other services (Google Translate, Google reCAPTCHA, YouTube) and depend on their accessibility.
>
> #### Getting information in another format
>
> If you need information from this website in a different format, such as accessible PDF, large print or plain text, contact us through [feedback page link] or [email address]. We'll reply within [number] working days.
>
> #### Reporting accessibility problems
>
> If you find a problem that isn't listed here, or think we're not meeting accessibility requirements, tell us through [feedback page link] or [email address]. Please include the page address and what went wrong.
>
> [If your country has an enforcement body: "If you're not happy with how we respond, contact [enforcement body and link]."]
>
> #### How we tested this website
>
> This website runs on ClimWeb [version]. It was tested with automated tools (axe-core) and keyboard checks on [date]. [Add screen reader testing once done: "We also tested it with [screen readers] on [date]."]
>
> This statement was prepared on [date] and last reviewed on [date].
